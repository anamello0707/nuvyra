Privacy policy

Nortia desktop app (previously called Nuvyra and Applyfly) · Last updated 9 October 2026

In short

Nortia is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. It has no servers, no user accounts and no tracking. Everything it stores stays on your computer, in the job search folder you choose and in the app's settings folder.

What Nortia stores, and where

  • Your job list (companies, roles, statuses, contacts, notes) in your job search folder.
  • Job ads you save, as Word files in that folder.
  • Settings, including the sign-in for connected mailboxes, in the app's settings folder on your computer. Mailbox sign-ins are encrypted with your operating system's secure storage.

None of this is sent to the maker of Nortia or to anyone else.

Email (Gmail and Microsoft Outlook / Hotmail)

If you choose to connect a mailbox, Nortia asks Google or Microsoft for read-only access (Gmail: gmail.readonly; Microsoft: Mail.Read). Nortia uses it only to:

  • find the emails about the job applications in your list (for example confirmations, interview invitations and answers) and suggest a new status for the application;
  • read the job alert emails you receive from job sites (such as Finn and LinkedIn) and show those jobs as suggestions.

Emails go directly from Google or Microsoft to Nortia on your computer. Nortia saves only the subject, sender, date and a short preview of the emails that match an application, and the job ad links from job alert emails. It never sends, changes or deletes emails, never shares email data with anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models. You can disconnect a mailbox at any time in Nortia's settings, and you can also remove Nortia's access in your Google or Microsoft account.

Nortia's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Web pages

When you add a job ad link, Nortia opens that page to read the job ad, just like a web browser would. Nortia also downloads a public skills list from GitHub to compare job ads with your CVs.

Optional AI key

If you choose to add your own AI provider key in the advanced settings, the text of a job ad you ask Nortia to read is sent to that provider (Anthropic or OpenAI) under your own account. Without a key, no AI service is used.
