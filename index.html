Applyfly

A free desktop app that keeps your job search organised.

  • Save job ads from a link (Finn, LinkedIn, company sites), each with its own folder.
  • Find your best CV for each job and see which skills are missing.
  • Tailor your CV in Word with tracked changes, and save it as PDF.
  • Follow up: see the emails about each application and get a hint when the status changes.
  • Suggested jobs from the job alerts you already receive by email.

Your data stays with you

Applyfly runs on your own computer. Your job list, CVs and folders stay there. Applyfly has no servers and never sees your data. If you connect a mailbox, Applyfly only reads it (it never sends, changes or deletes emails).
